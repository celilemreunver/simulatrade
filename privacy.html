Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Zaman Makinesi ("Time Machine") is an informational and entertainment app that compares the purchasing power of money in the past and today.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data that identifies you. There are no accounts or sign-ups. Your calculations are processed only on your device and are never sent to us.

Price data

The app may download the latest price list from this website (celilemreunver.github.io). No personal information is sent; as with any internet request, the hosting provider (GitHub) can see your device's IP address.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To verify the status of your purchase, the RevenueCat service processes an anonymous, device-specific identifier and your purchase records.

Advertising and tracking

No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are used.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
